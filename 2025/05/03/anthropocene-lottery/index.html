An Anthropocene Lottery

There is No Oracle Here

Please draw a lot for the Anthropocene.

This is a lot-drawing ritual with no known maker, no presiding god, no guarantee of fairness.

Please draw a lot. Please try again. Please live with the outcome.

Disclaimer: This is an experiment in environmental humanities, posthumanist thought, and ecological critique. Some lines draw inspiration from “life-ending memes” popular on Chinese social media; lines in quotation marks are direct references or adaptations from the translated writings of Shi Tiesheng. It is not meant as prophecy. There are 20 endings with one special “true ending.”
