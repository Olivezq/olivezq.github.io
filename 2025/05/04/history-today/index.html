Anthropocene:Today in History

The Anthropocene refers to an era in which human activities have become a significant geological force shaping the Earth’s systems. From the Industrial Revolution to today, every day has been marked by events that have left their imprint on this planet.

This page documents events that occurred on each calendar date during the Anthropocene, drawing from published sources across fields such as climate, environment, ecology, and socio-technical change.

As the data has been collected personally by the author, some major events may be missing, while seemingly minor yet meaningful moments are included. These entries may not always be “official,” but they are nonetheless part of the narrative of the Anthropocene.
