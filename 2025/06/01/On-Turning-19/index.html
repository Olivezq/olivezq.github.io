On Turning 19

有的人可能会说，哎主包怎么到了大学大变（谐音）样了啊，感觉变得很深刻嘛，文风和以前完全不一样嘛，

其实并非如此。实则不然。

主包说中文还是这个样子。

首先我想要感谢一下阿瓜sic老师为这个网站头像形象绘制的生贺图！实在是太美丽了（她甚至设计了一套全新的蝴蝶翅膀一样的礼服！还有一大块触手蛋糕！），简直不是稿件而是艺术品。

看着这个网站一点点丰满也是件美式。之前一直用的是一种极简主题，今年换成了这样式的，虽然deploy变慢但我实在太心水这个了。

讲道理我们高中就这么浑浑噩噩过去了，也不知道干了什么就18岁了，然后就19岁了。

趁这个机会说一下网站之后的安排：暑假我可能会上传一部小说，这取决于我接下来的一周能不能写完。然后会放一个Live2D上来！真的会很酷！（如果它不会进一步拖慢deploy的话）其他的像reflections什么的还是照常随缘发。

下面是英文版（并非如此实则不然）：

Happy to announce that———

It’s my 19th birthday!

Didn’t really enjoy birthdays bcz everytime I’ll wonder how does a birthday matter so much, like it’s just a normal day that randomly I was born and that doesn’t change anything in the years to come. But nevertheless, I decide to go follow some social norms and, so, this is an announcement. Sorry for that it’s not about achievements (well I guess living on this planet for 19 years could also count as an achievement, sort of…?).

I want to thank 阿瓜sic for drawing this birthday illustration for my site avatar! It’s honestly so beautiful (she even designed an entire butterfly-wing-style dress and a massive tentacle cake!), it’s not just a commission but a piece of art.

For this year, I also really appreciate how people I met made me included in communities. I also would like to give thanks to people who accept me or reject me, bcz they’ve been guiding me towards a path that I should/eventually will be going, they either cleared my way or pointed a new direction.

I know my writings are probably dark and dense to read (for the last few years), but it’s just my style and doesn’t mean I myself am ‘damp’, I was just trying to be honest and face what I can observe. I also write in a more casual style when in Chinese so I guess it’s also somehow a language thing. But anyway thanks for reading and following, I know it’s sometimes hard to read my writings and I appreciate if anyone paid attention and took time to understand.

This post is rather personal so I’ll keep it short.
