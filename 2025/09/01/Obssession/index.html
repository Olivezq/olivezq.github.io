执念 | Obsessions

中文版

不知描述过多少次飞机上的情景。不知坐过多少次厦门航空。

夜晚降临时，雨水开始倒映一路的橙色灯光。渐渐这光也被飞机抛走，火山爆发的余光似的坠在后面了。好像这一段故事还没处理完，还有未道尽的话语，就翻篇了。只能不断在思绪中纠缠，无法在现实中改变。

正如我昨天说：“无法将一段记忆笼统概括。不能称初中的一切为‘苦难’，却也不够认为它是’完全快乐‘的。’极端‘总会导致自欺欺人。但是，如果我每晚都会梦到初中和初中的同学们，那我要质疑我现在做的一些事情的动机了。”人总是对习以为常的反常不够敏感，所以我到现在才发现自己的梦是如此同质化。这可能意味初中对我的影响比我想象中大。但无论如何探索脑中的杂念，都只是在倾听自己的心跳罢了。我回不到也改变不了过去，过去已经被葬在地下，生者仅可在墓前沉思。

一阵混杂机械音的听不清歌词的童声响起，然后所有声音听了号令一般徒然增大，钻进云层。

小时候坐白天的飞机时，会很盼望穿过云。后来发现云中是白花花一片，感觉湿湿的。接着又开始幻想云上有哪些神仙，云间穿梭时怎么找准方向。

这次天和地没什么区别了。它同样有星星点点的光，不过是属于自然的闪电。蓬勃的紫喷涌而出。每闪一下，背光的云层就暴露出一块黑影。和恐怖片里的跳杀差不多。

和往常一样，我翻开杂志。2023年，厦门航空在A321neo机型中掺入10%由餐厨废油炼制的可持续航空燃料（SAF）后，进行了一次商业航班飞行。在Deepseek的文风中，我除此以外几乎什么也没读出来。再往后几页，《资本的扩张》《江南器物志》等等的书籍推荐，都显得其中的《爱伦坡小说全集》格格不入了。

多么美丽的地球。人们赞颂。无关看到的是表面还是深层，不管真诚或敷衍。

黑云像破碎的棉絮那样排列。长久的淡紫背景来自间隙中能窥见的城市灯光。几条穿戴珠宝的蛇蜿蜒曲折，一寸一寸的土地被勾勒包围。只有这样才能看见全貌，璀璨的一条黑舞裙上原来飘浮堆积已久的尘埃。可是这样又听不见点缀的悲喜了。谁在这天死去，谁又在今晚发现恨的人没死成？终于知晓错综复杂的棋局是如何排布，依然无法完全照顾到每颗棋子。人们面面相觑，喊，拥护光吧！

落地时它不再播放我初中时听的《爱在天际》了。

天明天依旧放晴。拥护光吧。

English Version

I don’t know how many times I have described the scenes on the plane. I don’t know how many times I have flown with Xiamen Airlines.

When night fell, the rain began to reflect the orange lights along the road. Gradually this light was also thrown away by the plane, falling behind like the afterglow of a volcanic eruption. It felt as if this part of the story had not yet been dealt with, there are still words left unsaid, and then the page is turned. One can only be entangled in thoughts again and again, unable to change anything in reality.

Just as I said yesterday: “A memory cannot be generalized. One cannot call all of secondary school ‘suffering,’ but neither is it enough to think of it as ‘completely happy.’ ‘Extremes’ will always lead to self-deception. However, if I dream of my secondary school and my classmates there every night, then I must question the motives of some of the things I am doing now.” People are always not sensitive enough to the abnormal in the habitual, so only now do I realize that my dreams are so homogeneous. This may mean that secondary school influenced me more than I had imagined. But no matter how I explore the clutter in my mind, it is only listening to my own heartbeat. I could not return to nor change the past, the past has already been buried underground, and the living can only contemplate in front of the grave.

A burst of children’s voices, mixed with mechanical sounds and indistinguishable lyrics, suddenly arose, and then all the sounds, as if following an order, suddenly grew louder, boring into the clouds.

When I was a child and took planes during the day, I would look forward to passing through the clouds. Later I found that inside the clouds it was all a whiteness, feeling damp. Then I began to imagine what immortals might be above the clouds, and how they would find their way while shuttling among them.

This time the sky and the earth made no difference. It also had specks of light, but they belonged to natural lightning. Vigorous purple spurted out. Each time it flashed, the backlit clouds exposed a patch of black shadow. It was much like the jump scares in horror films.

As usual, I opened the magazine. In 2023, after mixing 10% sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) refined from kitchen waste oil into the A321neo aircraft, Xiamen Airlines carried out a commercial flight. In Deepseek’s style, I could hardly read anything else out of it. A few pages later, book recommendations like The Expansion of Capital, Jiangnan Records of Artifacts, and so on, all made The Complete Stories of Edgar Allan Poe seem quite out of place among them.

What a beautiful Earth. People praise. Regardless of whether what they see is surface or depth, no matter sincere or perfunctory.

The black clouds were arranged like torn cotton. The long pale purple background came from the city lights visible through the gaps. A few jewel-wearing snakes wound and twisted, each inch of land outlined and encircled. Only in this way could the whole picture be seen: on a dazzling black dancing dress, the dust that had long floated and accumulated. But in this way, the sorrows and joys that dotted it could no longer be heard. Who died under this sky, and who tonight discovered that the person they hated did not die after all? Finally knowing how the intricate chessboard was arranged, still unable to fully take care of every piece. People exchange glances, shout, uphold the light!

When landing, it no longer played the Love in the Sky I heard in secondary school.

The sky tomorrow will still be clear. Uphold the light.

A/N

一些碎碎念……如题所示，混乱的只是未曾发现的执念，我并不怀念初中。其实也不是深刻的执念，隐隐约约有点感觉罢了。

有的人可能会觉得“人们面面相觑，喊，拥护光吧！”是一种号召/呼吁，实则不然，我本来想体现的是一种盲目的追随，就是人们不管怎么样都在呐喊，一切都乱成一锅粥了但只会喊“拥护光”，但是什么光，怎么拥护，一概不知。但是我没有展开去体现所以可能这没有那么明显。它主要是和前面的“多么美丽的地球。人们赞颂。不管真诚或敷衍”呼应，都是一种表面上的一致。结尾说“拥护光吧”，是要与前面有语境的落差，是明知荒谬仍要参与的被动。

最喜欢（感觉妙手偶得）的一句话是“可是这样又听不见点缀的悲喜了。谁在这天死去，谁又在今晚发现恨的人没死成？”。“谁在这天死去”，按常理应该对应的是前文的“悲”，但是后面“谁又在今晚发现恨的人没死成”来了一个反转，使得有人死去反而变成“喜”。再反过来，存活本应是“喜”，对某些人却是“悲”。灵感来源是前几天我想到“可是想活的没活成，想死的没死成”一句，这里进行了更委婉的措辞。这样在体现“几家欢喜几家愁”的同时，也可以体现悲喜并不是命运本身决定的，而是立场决定的。

“这次天和地没什么区别了”这两段呢，可以当作童年与成长的落差，但也可以当作“所谓高处也不一定比低处美好”这样的隐喻。这里其实就可以看出来了，这篇其实我是想有一个双线结构：主线是坐飞机时联想的宏观环境，支线是我对初中的感慨。两支线都较为混乱、未解，伴随着人的“执念”。

好的说点日常的，我之前说的小说没写完（因为我又想到了几个很天才的故事……）。Live2D的话因为网站放不下了所以只能放弃了（白旗）。8月没有更新因为我参加了几个绘画接龙呵呵，没有时间。

Some rambling thoughts… As the title suggests, what is chaotic is only the unnoticed obsession. I do not miss my secondary school. Actually it is not even a deep obsession, just a faint and vague feeling.

Some people may think that “People exchange glances, shout, uphold the light!” is a kind of call/appeal, but it is not so. What I originally wanted to show was a kind of blind following, that people, no matter what, are shouting, everything has turned into a mess but they only keep shouting “Uphold the light,” yet what light, how to uphold, they know nothing at all (most of the time). But I did not expand on it to show this, so perhaps it is not so obvious. It is mainly meant to echo the earlier “What a beautiful Earth. People praise. Regardless of whether sincere or perfunctory,” both being a kind of superficial unanimity. The ending line “Uphold the light” is meant to create a contextual gap with what came before: it is a passive participation, knowing it is absurd yet still taking part.

The sentence I like most (it feels like a lucky stroke) is “But in this way, the sorrows and joys that dotted it could no longer be heard. Who died under this sky, and who tonight discovered that the person they hated did not die after all?” “Who died under this sky”: by convention it ought to correspond to the earlier “sorrow.” But then the latter part, “who tonight discovered that the person they hated did not die after all,” makes a reversal, so that someone’s death instead turns into “joy.” Conversely, survival, which ought to be “joy,” for some people becomes “sorrow.” The inspiration came from something I thought of a few days ago: “But those who wanted to live did not manage to, those who wanted to die did not manage to.” Here I put it into more tactful wording. In this way, while it reflects the idea of “some rejoice, some grieve,” it also shows that sorrow and joy are not determined by fate itself, but by one’s standpoint.

As for the part “This time the sky and the earth made no difference,” it can be taken as the gap between childhood and growing up, but it can also be taken as the metaphor that “what is called the heights is not necessarily more beautiful than what is below.” Here it can already be seen that this piece was actually meant to have a dual-line structure: the main line is the associations of the macro environment while on the plane, and the sub-line is my reflections on secondary school. Both lines are rather chaotic, unresolved, accompanied by human “obsessions.”

Alright, to talk about some everyday things: the novel I mentioned before is not finished (because I thought of a few more genius stories again…). As for Live2D, since the website couldn’t hold it anymore, I had to give it up (white flag). There was no update in August because I joined an art relay haha, and didn’t have time.
