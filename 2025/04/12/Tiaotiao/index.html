条条 Tiaotiao (Lines)

An excerpt from an experimental fiction exploring human-nature entanglements. Leng Qinghe appears as a victim and a secondary figure, a fragile body seen from the distance of a witness. This excerpt forms the opening of Zhaohun (Calling Souls), the first part of the Weaving Ghosts trilogy that set amid the ruins of the Sixth Mass Extinction.
This excerpt contains depictions of death and violence.

中文版

冷青禾身上背负五条人命。

两条祖父母，两条父母，一条弟弟。是的，是亲手杀死的，都在那条莲叶连绵的瑞蓝河上。他们的面庞那样清淡，生气被河水吸干。看不见水底，望不到巫村，只有反覆无常枕在大腿上的尸体，波光粼粼。

他的人生就是关于“一条条”的悲怆。名字是条状的禾稻，人是细瘦长条，还有一条河，五条人命，几十条新闻和无数条令人麻木的事件。

天已经黑了。花盆向窗外倒去，条条枝桠稀稀拉拉挂在架上。

冷青禾觉得不对，这不对了。

“是你杀死了你的家人”“请你看看你的弟弟吧”诸如此类的话又在通过空气中隐形的话筒放大到他耳中，他睁大眼想看清男人托举着的弟弟的模样。弟弟那样颓唐地淌，四肢一条条，枯萎的水鬼蕉，面中一团晦暗的模糊。像蛔虫打结，毛线。嘈杂的人声。灰蒙蒙的呼吸。

这条始终记不起弟弟相貌的冷青禾，在这天身上出现五条刀伤，他倒在血泊中。青灰的眼白对着屋顶之外的黑夜，在莲河下挣扎的鱼被翻滚的血包围，最终一动不动任由纷扰。

English Version

Leng Qinghe bore five lines of death on his back.

Two lines for his grandparents, two for his parents, one for his younger brother. Yes, all killed by his own hands, there on that rippling blue river with densy lotus leaves. Their faces were bland, drained of life by water. The riverbed was invisible, the village unreachable; only were the corpses resting erratically on his lap, river shimmering under the flickering light.

His life was a grief of “lines.” Name represented a slender stalk of rice; body was a thin, narrow strip; and one line of river, five lines of death, dozens of news headlines, countless numbing events.

The sky had darkened. Flowerpots fell outside the window, branches hanging down in thin, sparse lines along the trellis.

Leng Qinghe felt something was wrong, this was wrong.

“It’s you who killed your family.”“Please take a look at your brother.” These voices swelled through invisible loudspeakers in the air again, he opened his eyes wide, wanting to see the brother held aloft by the man. But the brother drifted languid and limp, limbs trailing down like withered water bananas, his face a blurred, dark mass. Like knots of worms, tangled yarn. A cacophony of voices. Breaths gray and dull.

This line of Leng Qinghe who could never recall his brother’s face, found five lines of slashes appear across his own body this day, collapsed in a pool of blood. Gray-white eyes stared beyond the roof into the night, under the river of lotus, struggling fish was surrounded by swirling blood, until it finally lay still, surrendering to the chaos.
